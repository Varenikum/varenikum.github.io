# Flappy Pegasus

Small pixel game about a pegasus that just wants to keep flying.
Tap to go up, don't hit the ground, don't hit the robots. That's pretty much it.

Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.SparkleCorp.FlappyPegasus

What's in there:

- 9 ponies you can unlock with coins
- day, night, twilight and space
- three robots (T-800, T-1000 and T-228) who really want you to stop
- achievements and a leaderboard via Google Play Games

The first version was made in Unity, now the whole thing is rewritten in Godot.
If something broke after the update, write me and I'll fix it.
